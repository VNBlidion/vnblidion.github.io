1. Getting started

  • Why does Cheery need access to my photos?Cheery sorts and cleans your photo library right on your phone, so it needs to see your photos and videos. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Cheery only shows some of my photos.You may have allowed access to selected photos only. On iPhone, go to Settings › Apps › Cheery › Photos and choose Full Access. On Android, go to Settings › Apps › Cheery › Permissions › Photos and videos and choose Allow all.
  • My daily reminder doesn't appear.Check that notifications for Cheery are turned on in your phone's settings, then pick a reminder time in Cheery's Settings.

2. Deleting and restoring photos

  • Where do removed photos go?Into Cheery's Trash. Nothing is deleted from your phone until you empty the Trash and confirm. Tap any photo in the Trash to keep it.
  • I emptied the Trash by mistake.Your phone usually keeps deleted photos for about 30 days. On iPhone, open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted. On Android, open Google Photos or your Gallery app and look in Trash or Bin.
  • I emptied the Trash but my storage barely changed.Your phone keeps deleted photos for a while before freeing the space. To free it now, also empty Recently Deleted in Photos on iPhone, or the Trash in Google Photos or Gallery on Android.

3. Cheery Pro and purchases

  • What does Cheery Pro include?Large media, Custom clean, every year in Journal, and no ads. Choose a yearly plan with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, a monthly plan, or a 1-week or 1-month pass that never renews.
  • How do I restore Pro on a new phone?Open the Cheery Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy Pro.
  • How do I cancel my subscription?On iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Cheery. On Android, open Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Passes don't renew, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • How do I get a refund?Apple and Google handle payments, so they also handle refunds. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history and choose Request a refund.

4. Privacy and ads

  • Are my photos uploaded?No. Cheery works on your phone, with no account and no cloud. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
  • How do I turn off usage stats or crash reports?Open Cheery and go to Settings › Data & privacy. You can change these anytime.
  • Ads in the free versionAfter you finish a pile, the free version may show one full-screen ad, at most once every few minutes. Ads never appear while you swipe. Cheery Pro removes all ads. To limit ad personalization, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on iPhone, or Settings › Google › Ads on Android.
