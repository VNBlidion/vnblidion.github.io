the details

1. What Cheery accesses

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cheery app (“Cheery”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. With your permission, Cheery accesses the following, on your device only:

  • Photos and videosCheery reads your photo and video library so you can view, sort and delete items. You can give access to your full library or to selected photos only, and change it at any time in your phone’s settings or in Cheery under Settings › Data & privacy.
  • Photo locationIf your photos contain location information (where a picture was taken), Cheery reads it to group your photos by place in Memories. Cheery does not track your current location.
  • NotificationsIf you turn on the daily reminder, Cheery asks to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled by your phone itself, with no server.

2. On-device processing

To find similar photos, recognize what a photo shows and organize your memories, Cheery uses Google ML Kit image labeling and face detection. This analysis runs entirely on your device, and your photos are not sent anywhere for it.

Face detection only finds whether and where faces appear in a photo. It does not identify who a person is, and Cheery does not create or store facial recognition data.

3. Crash reports and usage stats

Cheery uses Google Firebase to receive anonymous crash reports and, only if you agree, usage stats. Neither ever includes your photos or videos.

  • Crash reportsOn by default. When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends the error type, a technical trace of the code, your device model, Android version, app version, app language, whether Pro is on, a rough library size (for example “1k–10k items”), the screen that was open and a random installation ID. File paths and file names are removed before anything is sent. Turn this off in Settings › Data & privacy › Share crash reports.
  • Usage statsOff until you agree. Cheery asks once, when you first set it up. If you say yes, it sends counts and fixed names only: which tools you open, how many photos you kept, let go and deleted, how much space was freed, which kinds of memories you open, your streak length, when the Pro screen is shown and when a purchase is made, and the names of screens. Firebase also records basic details such as a random app instance ID, device model, app version and your approximate country or region. Turn this off in Settings › Data & privacy › Share usage stats; turning it off also resets the stats gathered on your phone.
  • Never sentPhotos, videos, thumbnails, file names, file paths, albums, places, photo dates, image labels and face data are never sent, in crash reports or in usage stats.
Crash reports and usage stats never include your advertising ID and are never used for advertising. Firebase keeps crash reports for up to 90 days and usage stats for a limited period. Google processes this data on our behalf, and it may be stored on servers outside your country.

4. Ads in the free version

The free version of Cheery shows ads served by Google AdMob. Cheery Pro removes all ads.

  • Where ads appearOne full-screen ad may appear after you finish a pile of photos, at most once every few minutes. Ads never appear while you are swiping, when you open Cheery or when you close it.
  • What AdMob receivesYour device’s advertising ID, your IP address (which Google uses to estimate your approximate location, such as your city or country), basic device and app information, and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps. AdMob never receives your photos, videos, file names or the locations stored in your photos.
  • How Google uses itTo show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, prevent fraud and, only if you agree, personalize them. Google uses this data under its own privacy policy, so under Google Play’s rules this counts as sharing data with a third party.
  • Your consentIn the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Cheery shows Google’s consent form before any personalized ads, and you can change your answer later from Cheery’s settings. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone’s settings under Google › Ads.
We do not pick individual ads, and we never receive your advertising ID or your location. Ads in Cheery are not directed at children.

5. Cheery Pro and payments

  • PaymentsCheery Pro is sold through Google Play, which processes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase went through and which plan it was.
  • PlansCheery Pro comes in four plans. Yearly and Monthly are subscriptions that renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. The 1-week and 1-month passes are paid once, give you Pro for that period and then end on their own. They never renew and never charge you again. Prices are shown in the app and on Google Play before you buy.
  • Free trialNew subscribers to the Yearly plan get a 3-day free trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged and the subscription renews each year. If notifications are on, Cheery reminds you on your phone one day before the trial ends. The other plans have no free trial.
  • Cancelling and refundsYou can cancel a subscription anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again. Passes need no cancelling. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy.
  • On your phoneYour Pro status is saved on your phone and checked with Google Play, for example when you restore a purchase on a new phone.
  • Usage statsIf you agreed to share usage stats, Cheery also records when the Pro screen is shown and when a purchase is started or completed, by plan name only.

6. What stays on your phone

Cheery saves a small amount of information on your device so the app works as expected:

  • SettingsLanguage, reminder time and other preferences.
  • Streak and JournalYour streak and your cleanup history.
  • TrashJust a list of what you removed.
  • Cheery ProWhether you have Pro, if you bought it.
  • Privacy choicesWhether you share crash reports and usage stats.
  • Ad consentYour answer to the ads consent form, saved on your phone by Google’s consent tool.
This information stays on your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time in Settings › Data & privacy › Erase app data, or by uninstalling the app. Erasing app data never deletes your photos.

7. Deleting photos

Let go only adds a photo to Cheery’s Trash list. Nothing is deleted until you tap Delete there and confirm in your phone’s own delete dialog.

Depending on your phone, deleted items may be kept for a while in your system’s own trash or Recently deleted folder. This is managed by your phone, not by Cheery.

8. Third-party services

  • Google ML KitProcesses your images on your device, as described above. To keep its features working, ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device and app details, performance metrics and diagnostics. Your photos are never included. See the Google ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play BillingIf you buy Cheery Pro, the payment is processed by Google Play. We never receive or see your payment card details; Google Play only tells the app whether a purchase went through. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Google FirebaseReceives the crash reports and usage stats described above. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMobShows ads in the free version, as described in section 4. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
Cheery uses no other analytics, advertising or tracking services. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

9. Sharing and selling

We never sell your photos or personal information. Crash reports and usage stats go to Google Firebase, which processes them only on our behalf. In the free version, Google AdMob receives the ad data described in section 4 and uses it under Google’s own policies. That is the only data Cheery shares with a third party, and buying Cheery Pro stops it. Your photos and videos are never collected or shared.

10. Children’s privacy

Cheery is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Cheery are not directed at children.

11. Your choices

  • Photo accessAllow, limit or turn off photo access at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Share crash reportsTurn them on or off in Settings › Data & privacy.
  • Share usage statsTurn them on or off in Settings › Data & privacy. They stay off unless you agree.
  • Ad personalizationChange your consent from Cheery’s settings, or reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your phone’s settings under Google › Ads.
  • Remove adsBuy Cheery Pro to stop all ads and the ad data that comes with them.
  • Delete your dataEmail us and we will delete the crash reports and usage stats linked to your installation within 30 days. Ad data is held by Google: use your phone’s ad settings or Google’s ad controls.
  • Daily reminderTurn it on or off in Cheery or in your phone’s notification settings.
  • App dataErase all of Cheery’s data in Settings › Data & privacy.
  • UninstallUninstalling the app stops all use of your information.
