Cheery Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cheery app ("Cheery", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information when you use it on your device. Cheery helps you review, organize and clean up the photos and videos on your phone.

In short: Cheery works on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and your photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to any server. We do not sell your data.

1. Information the app accesses

With your permission, Cheery accesses the following on your device only:

  • Photos and videos. Cheery reads your photo and video library so you can view, sort and delete items. You can grant access to your full library or to selected photos only, and you can change this at any time in your device settings or in Cheery under Settings → Data & privacy.
  • Photo location data. If your photos contain location information (for example, where a picture was taken), Cheery reads it to group your photos by place in Memories. This location data is read from your photos on your device. Cheery does not track your current location.
  • Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder, Cheery asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device and requires no server.

2. On-device processing

To find similar photos, recognize what a photo contains, and organize your memories, Cheery uses Google ML Kit image labeling and face detection. This analysis runs entirely on your device. Your photos are not sent anywhere for this processing. Face detection only finds whether and where faces appear in a photo. It does not identify who a person is, and Cheery does not create or store facial recognition templates.

3. Information stored on your device

Cheery saves a small amount of information locally on your device so the app works as expected, including:

  • Your settings, such as language, reminder time and preferences;
  • Your cleaning streak, Journal and cleanup history;
  • The list of items you chose to remove (the Trash list);
  • Your Cheery Pro status, if you have purchased it.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. You can erase it at any time in Settings → Data & privacy → Erase app data, or by uninstalling the app. Erasing app data never deletes your photos.

4. Deleting photos

Cheery deletes photos and videos only when you ask it to and confirm. Depending on your device, deleted items may be moved to your device's own trash or recycle bin, where you may be able to recover them for a limited time.

5. Third-party services

  • Google ML Kit. ML Kit processes your images on your device, as described above. To keep its features working, ML Kit may send Google limited technical information, such as device and app details, performance metrics and diagnostics. Your photos are not included. See Google ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing. If you buy Cheery Pro, the payment is processed by Google Play. We never receive or see your payment card details. Google Play only tells the app whether a purchase succeeded. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Cheery does not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. We do not collect your photos, videos or personal data on our servers, because Cheery does not have servers that store user data.

7. Children's privacy

Cheery is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • You can allow, limit or turn off photo access at any time in your device settings.
  • You can turn the daily reminder on or off in the app or in your device's notification settings.
  • You can erase all of Cheery's app data in Settings → Data & privacy.
  • You can stop all use of your information by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact us
